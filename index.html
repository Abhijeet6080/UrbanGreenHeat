🔬 Methodology

1

Satellite Imagery

Landsat 8/9 Collection 2, Level-2 surface reflectance and surface temperature products from USGS.

2

Cloud & Shadow Masking

The QA_PIXEL band is used to remove cloudy and cloud-shadowed pixels before analysis.

3

NDVI Calculation

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index computed from near-infrared and red bands. Range: −1 to +1.

4

LST Extraction

Land Surface Temperature derived from the thermal band (ST_B10), converted from Kelvin to Celsius.

5

Heat Hotspot Detection

Hotspots are identified using percentile-based thresholds (default: 90th percentile) of the LST distribution within the study area. This is a relative threshold, not a universal cutoff.

6

NDVI–LST Relationship

Scatter analysis reveals the inverse correlation typically observed between vegetation cover and surface temperature.

7

Priority Greening Areas

Locations with high surface temperature AND low vegetation are flagged as analytical priority areas for potential greening.

📡 Data Sources

🛰️
USGS Landsat 8/9
Collection 2 Level-2 Surface Reflectance (SR) and Surface Temperature (ST) products. Spatial resolution: 30m (SR), 100m resampled to 30m (ST).
🌍
Google Earth Engine
Cloud computing platform used to process satellite imagery, calculate indices, and export geospatial results.
🗺️
OpenStreetMap
Base map tiles providing geographic context. © OpenStreetMap contributors.

⚠️ Important Notes

  • LST measures surface temperature, not near-surface air temperature.
  • NDVI classification thresholds may vary by climate and region.
  • Hotspot detection uses relative (percentile) thresholds within the study area.
  • Priority greening areas are analytical outputs, not prescriptive recommendations.
